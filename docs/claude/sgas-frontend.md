Result: (a) the cells' `border-right` lines drew white verticals THROUGH each bar, (b) the cells (with the drag-create mousedown handlers) sat on top and stole clicks → clicking a bar started a 1-day cell drag → CreateModal, never reaching the bar's onClick→edit drawer. LESSON: in a CSS grid where cells are position:relative and bars overlap them, bars MUST be position:relative + higher z-index or they're non-interactive and get sliced by cell borders.
